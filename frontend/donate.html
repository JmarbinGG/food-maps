Do Good Food Maps.

Too many East Bay families don't know where to find free, nutritious food, even when it's just around the corner. Do Good Food Maps, a project of the All Good Living Foundation, puts local food pantries, community closets, and other food resources on one easy-to-use map, so no family has to go hungry for lack of information.

Give today and help put good food on the map.

Your gift helps us keep the map accurate and up to date, add new food distributors, secure food across the Bay Area, and connect families in need with healthy food when they need it most. Every dollar goes toward closing the gap between available food and the people who need it.
